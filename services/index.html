Services

Four ways to engage, each one bounded.

Fixed scope, fixed price, phased. You can stop after any phase and keep everything produced in it.

PhaseYou receiveTypical duration
Assess and adviseWritten findings, inventory, prioritized problem list, and a recommendation that may be to do nothing 1 to 2 weeks
DesignTopology, addressing plan, channel and power plan, segmentation model, bill of materials with supplier and date per line, implementation runbook2 to 4 weeks
DeployBench staging, configuration, supervised cabling by a licensed contractor, midweek cutover with written rollback, validation against the designScoped per site
SupportMonitoring, firmware lifecycle, configuration changes, quarterly review, incident response within agreed severity and hours Monthly, cancellable

What is included in a design

  • Site survey with measured signal, interference and airtime, per space
  • Topology and addressing that is still readable in ten years
  • Segmentation model by role, with the routing decisions written down
  • Channel and transmit power plan, per radio, per band
  • Bill of materials at supplier price with the URL and date on every line
  • An implementation runbook another engineer could build from

What is deliberately excluded

  • Physical cabling, conduit and mounting. A licensed contractor does that work and you engage them directly
  • Help desk, endpoint management, email and user support. That is a different business and I am not in it
  • Anything I cannot document well enough for you to take over

ButeraNet Solutions LLC is not a licensed Hawaii contractor and does not advertise contracting services. Cabling and physical installation are quoted and performed by licensed contractors.

How the support plan is bounded

An open-ended retainer is how a small practice quietly goes out of business. A support plan here states four numbers, and everything outside them is quoted as project work.

SITES

How many sites

Named explicitly. A new campus is a new project, priced as one.

HOURS

Which hours

Coverage window stated as real clock hours, including what after-hours means.

SEVERITY

What counts as urgent

Severity tiers with a response target attached to each one.

SCOPE

What is in scope

The network I built. Redesigns and additions are quoted, never absorbed.
